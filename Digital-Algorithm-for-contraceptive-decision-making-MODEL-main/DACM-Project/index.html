The Problem We're Solving

Contraceptive discontinuation rates exceed 40% in Sub-Saharan Africa due to critical gaps in personalized care.

Safety Risks

Generic advice ignores clinical contraindications like hypertension, migraines with aura, and DVT history, putting patients at risk.

Context Gap

Most tools ignore regional prevalence data and local availability, making recommendations irrelevant to African contexts.

Behavioral Blind Spot

Existing systems predict what's medically safe but miss what users will actually adhere to, leading to high discontinuation.

Privacy Concerns

Sensitive health data is often exposed, preventing open consultation and reducing trust in digital health tools.

What Makes DACM Different

A complete clinical decision support system built for real-world healthcare delivery in Africa.

WHO-Guideline Safety Engine

Rule-based clinical checks evaluate 12+ parameters to block contraindicated methods and flag critical risks automatically.

RAG-Powered Clinical Advice

ChromaDB retrieves relevant WHO MEC guidelines, and a Groq LLM synthesizes empathetic, evidence-based recommendations.

Behavioral Satisfaction ML

LightGBM model (0.919 AUC) predicts user adherence risk based on historical review patterns for proactive counseling.

Regional Context Integration

WHO Africa DHS prevalence data (1,161 records across 54 countries) provides localized counseling based on actual usage.

Zero-PII Privacy Architecture

SHA-256 identity hashing and contact masking ensure patient anonymity. Counselors see only what's needed for triage.

Blinded Counselor Dashboard

High-risk cases are auto-routed to counselors with secure telehealth scheduling. Full audit trail and admin oversight included.

How It Works

From patient query to clinical recommendation in seconds.
